RepKal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

RepKal ("the app", "we") is provided by Support RPM. You can contact us at contactwithus.info@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication – we never see or store your password).
  • Health and fitness information you enter: age, gender, height, weight, goals, activity level, food preference, weight log, workouts completed, water intake and foods you log.
  • Profile photo (optional): if you add one, a small copy is stored with your account so it shows on your other devices. You can remove it at any time.
  • App usage: the last day you used the app, how many days you have used it, and the app version, so we can understand how many people use RepKal and fix problems.
  • Advertising data: the app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address, and app interaction data to show and measure ads.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and calculate your calorie target, BMI, diet chart and progress.
  • To save your data so it is available if you change phones or reinstall the app.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores your account and fitness data on Google's servers.
  • Google AdMob serves ads. See how Google uses data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Notifications and permissions

If you turn on the daily workout reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are created on your phone. The camera or gallery is used only when you choose to add a profile photo.

5. Data security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access rules ensure that you can read and change only your own data.

6. How to delete your account and data

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account & data, then enter your password. Your account and all stored data (profile, logs, photo and usage record) are permanently deleted.
  • By email: if you can no longer use the app, email contactwithus.info@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my account" and we will delete your data within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Health disclaimer

Calorie, BMI and diet numbers are estimates for general fitness only and are not medical advice. Please consult a doctor before starting any exercise or diet programme, especially if you have a health condition.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
